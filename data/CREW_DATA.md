# Synthetic crew-roster data

These files are fictional POC data. Crew IDs are deliberately anonymous; do not replace them with real employee information without formal authority, access controls, and a privacy review.

## Files

- `crew_members.csv` is the availability and capability master. A crew member is eligible only when their role, section and equipment qualification match the duty, they are available, and their status is `available`.
- `crew_absences.csv` records planned leave, training or medical restrictions. An approved absence overrides apparent availability.
- `running_duties.csv` represents loco-pilot and guard demand generated by the timetable. `uncovered_absence` and `unassigned` rows are deliberate demo cases for the optimiser.

## Fatigue-aware assignment rules for the POC

1. Never assign a crew member who is absent, unavailable, unqualified, or already assigned to an overlapping duty.
2. Enforce locally configured minimum rest using `last_sign_off` and proposed duty start.
3. Do not exceed `max_duty_minutes` for the planning period.
4. Use `night_duties_last_7d` and `fatigue_band` only as penalties between eligible people.
5. Leave a duty without a compliant person as `manual_review` / `uncovered`.

For a real rollout, use the railway administration's authorised duty, rest, medical, competence and privacy rules.

## India-specific running-staff fields

`crew_members.csv` includes crew lobby, home station, medical status, route-learning validity, traction qualification, duty category, days away from headquarters, monthly periodic-rest hours and consecutive-night-duty count. `running_duties.csv` includes `duty_location` (`hq` or `outstation`).
